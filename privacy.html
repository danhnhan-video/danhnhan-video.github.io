Privacy Policy

DanhNhan Video Uploader · Last updated: 23 November 2026

DanhNhan Video Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload documentary videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains how the app handles Google user data.

1. Data the app accesses

The app requests a single Google OAuth permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. With it the app uploads video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and the publication schedule to the authorizing user's YouTube channel. The app does not access any other Google account data.

2. How the data is used

The permission is used only to publish videos that the owner has produced, to the owner's own channel. The app does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage

The app stores only the OAuth refresh token needed to keep the upload connection active. The token is kept in the owner's private Google Colab secrets storage and is never published. No YouTube or Google account data is copied to any other server.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, transfer or share Google user data with any third party.

5. Limited Use

DanhNhan Video Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/connections. After revocation the app can no longer upload videos.
