DanhNhan Video Uploader

A personal tool for publishing biography documentaries to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What this app does

DanhNhan Video Uploader is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel to publish short documentary videos about the lives of famous historical figures. The videos are produced by the owner from public-domain and openly licensed archival images, an original script, and a narration voice-over.

When a new video is ready, the tool uploads the video file, its title, description, tags and thumbnail to the owner's own YouTube channel, and can schedule the publication time.

How it uses Google data

  • The only permission requested is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (upload videos and set thumbnails).
  • The app acts only on the channel of the person who authorizes it (the owner).
  • It does not read, collect, sell or share any other Google user data.
